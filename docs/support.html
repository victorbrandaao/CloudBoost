CLOUDBOOST / SUPPORT

Keep your purchase private. Get back to playing.

Get private CloudBoost license help, recover your purchase and move PRO to a replacement Mac. Keep keys and receipts out of public issues.

  1. Keep the original purchase email and license key. Search your inbox for CloudBoost and Payhip; use the download link in the receipt to recover the purchase.
  2. If you still have the old Mac, stop your session and open Session Center → License & support → Release this Mac. Wait for server confirmation before activating on the replacement.
  3. If the old Mac is unavailable or release fails, request a private conversation with the CloudBoost maintainer in Discord. Never send keys, receipts or purchase emails to a public channel.
Open project Discord ↗Public bug report

If the invitation fails, open a public issue asking how to contact the maintainer privately. Include only the app version and a description of the problem; do not include your key, receipt or purchase email.

PRO 5 starts at US$12 once for one Mac. Higher payment is optional and includes the same features. Existing PRO 5 keys cover 5.x. Everyone coming from 4.x needs a new PRO 5 purchase; previous purchases remain valid in 4.x.
