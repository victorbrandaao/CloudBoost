The Sims 4 on Mac

Why is The Sims 4 slowing down your Mac?

The Sims 4 is one of the most played games on macOS. But as you add expansions, CC (Custom Content), and mods, the game can start stuttering heavily, making fast-forwarding impossible and loading screens endless.

Separating Hardware Pressure from Game Engine Limits

When The Sims 4 stutters on a MacBook, it is usually tied to system resources:

  • Thermal Throttling: Running The Sims 4 with high settings on a MacBook Air will quickly heat up the chassis. The Mac protects itself by throttling the CPU/GPU, instantly halving your framerate.
  • Memory Pressure (Mods & CC): Large amounts of Custom Content eat up RAM. If your Mac runs out of physical memory, it uses the SSD for "Swap Memory", causing massive stutter when moving the camera.
  • EA App / Origin Background Usage: The launcher can sometimes consume unnecessary CPU in the background, starving the game of resources.

What to check for The Sims 4 sessions

  • Check macOS Thermal Pressure to ensure your Mac isn't actively throttling due to heat.
  • Monitor System Memory Pressure to see if your CC folder is too large for your Mac's RAM.
  • Turn off Low Power Mode when playing on battery.
  • Close browser tabs and other applications before launching the game.
