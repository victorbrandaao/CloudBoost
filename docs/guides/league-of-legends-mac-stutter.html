League of Legends on Mac

Small interruptions can feel big in a match.

League can feel inconsistent on Mac when local pressure, Wi-Fi behavior, thermal state or background apps compete with the session.

What to check first

  • Turn off Low Power Mode before ranked sessions.
  • Close browser tabs, launchers and sync apps that wake during matches.
  • Prefer Ethernet or a stable Wi-Fi channel.
  • Watch jitter and packet loss when ping looks fine but input feels off.
  • Check thermal pressure on MacBooks during long sessions.

Where CloudBoost helps

CloudBoost does not change League itself. It helps keep the Mac session quieter and explains whether macOS-side pressure, Wi-Fi/AWDL behavior, VPN routing or background work may be contributing.
