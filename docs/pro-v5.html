Before you upgrade

Does CloudBoost guarantee lower ping or more FPS?

No. Measurements describe observed conditions. They do not measure every game server or prove an FPS gain. CloudBoost helps you diagnose issues and decide what to test next.

Will my existing PRO license work in version 5?

No. PRO 5 requires a new purchase from US$12. Your previous purchase remains valid for all 4.x releases.
