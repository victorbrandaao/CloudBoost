Privacy

Simple analytics, only with your permission.

CloudBoost does not sell visitor data, run advertising pixels, or build a personal profile from the website.

When analytics is enabled

The site asks before loading Google Analytics. If you allow it, we measure page visits and product actions such as downloads, PRO checkout opens, Discord support, Session Review requests and public reviews. These totals help us understand which pages, languages and communities lead people to the right place.

What we do not collect

CloudBoost does not send your license key, Session Proof, game activity, files, browser history or diagnostic data from the app through this website analytics setup.

Your choice

You can decline analytics in the prompt. To change a previous choice, clear this site's local storage in your browser and reload the page.

For product telemetry, see the Anonymous Stats setting inside CloudBoost. It is separate from this website preference.
