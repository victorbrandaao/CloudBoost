5.1 / WHAT’S NEW

What it can do

01

See app traffic

Find receive/send rates for apps and processes. Helpers are grouped where macOS allows attribution. Measurements stay on your Mac.

02

Choose an action

Open an app to pause its download or sync. Or ask an eligible third-party app to quit normally, then reopen it afterwards. Games and system services are protected.

03

Check what changed

Compare observed traffic before and after your chosen app exit. Network changes make the comparison inconclusive. Traffic volume alone is not proof of a lag cause.

04

Match your screen

Compare reported Hz with your selected stream FPS target. Preview a supported rate at the same resolution; unconfirmed changes revert after 15 seconds.

05

Remember your setup · PRO

Save a preset and stream FPS target for each game. PRO restores the saved preset when you select the profile. Display changes still need confirmation.

06

Share useful evidence · PRO

Copy a short session summary with measured numbers, without license keys, email, network addresses or full support logs.

Try it in four steps

  1. Open CloudBoost → Session Center before your game.
  2. Look at app traffic. Open a busy app and pause its download or sync from its own controls.
  3. If appropriate, request a normal app exit. Save work first; CloudBoost does not force-quit.
  4. Compare the observed sample, then reopen the app after playing. Check downloads in the app.

This is not a firewall, a per-app bandwidth cap or a new server route. CloudBoost does not measure actual game FPS. Keep your game or streaming overlay for server, decoding and rendering statistics. Results depend on your setup.
