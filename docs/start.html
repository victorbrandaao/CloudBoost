Install the preview

Open the DMG and drag CloudBoost into Applications. Launch the app and look for its icon in the macOS menu bar.

Choose what you play

Select your cloud service or game and begin with Balanced. You can use the basic Free workflow without an account.

Try a complete PRO session

Choose “Start 3-session PRO trial” in the app. Play for at least one minute, then stop the session to restore settings and review the result. No payment details are required.

A trial session counts after one minute. Free stays available after the three PRO sessions.

Before installing

CloudBoost is independently distributed and is not currently notarized with an Apple Developer ID. Read the Trust Center before opening it. macOS can ask for administrator authorization for selected system actions.
